# Customer-scoped Drive folders and access

New project folders live under a persistent Customer folder as `NN-<Project>`, numbered per Customer from the highest existing number, padded to at least two digits, and allocated durably so concurrent provisioning and retries preserve the same number and folder. Customer identity is shared across projects: existing names that match after trimming and case-folding are linked during migration; Sales and Product can select or create Customers, while only Lead SA/Admin can rename them. Existing flat project folders and provisioned folder paths are not automatically moved or renamed. We intentionally grant every SA team member Editor access across projects to preserve the existing manual workflow; Sales and Product retain their separate project-level access rules from ADR 0015. This supersedes ADR 0015 only regarding SA-team access scope; its other rules remain in effect, including Product access to Inventory.
